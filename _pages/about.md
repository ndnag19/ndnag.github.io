Prior to pursuing my MBA, I worked at ZS Associates, where I supported investment decisions and growth strategies for Fortune 500 healthcare organizations. My work involved developing forecasting and valuation frameworks, assessing commercial opportunities, and helping clients navigate complex strategic choices.

I began my career after graduating from the National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli, where I studied Metallurgical and Materials Engineering with a minor in Management. Over time, I found myself increasingly drawn to questions involving markets, investments, and strategic decision-making.

Through this website, I aim to share practical insights on financial modeling, due diligence, Excel productivity, and structured problem solving. My goal is to bridge the gap between academic finance concepts and their real-world application.
